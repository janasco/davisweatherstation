# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for security problems.

Report vulnerabilities privately to **contact@davisweatherstation.com** with:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce
- The affected page or feature
- Any relevant screenshots or logs, without secrets or personal data

We aim to acknowledge reports within a few business days and will keep you updated on remediation.

## Scope

In scope:

- The public website and its forms
- Data exposure or privacy issues
- Abuse or spam bypasses
- Cross-site scripting, injection, or request forgery

Out of scope:

- Denial-of-service testing or automated scanning that degrades the service
- Social engineering
- Third-party services (report those to the provider)
- Findings that require access to a user's device or account

## Safe harbour

We will not pursue legal action for good-faith research that respects this policy, avoids privacy violations, and does not disrupt the service. Please do not access, modify, or retain data that does not belong to you.
